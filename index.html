AboutClear explanations, lots of practice.

I teach Cambridge Computer Science in the classroom and online, from first lines of pseudocode to A Level programming and theory.

My lessons follow a simple loop: explain one idea, let students try a real exam-style question straight away, and give feedback before moving on. I focus heavily on exam technique: command words, mark schemes and the trace tables, flowcharts and SQL that students most often lose marks on.

Outside class I build my own teaching resources: an interactive revision website with self-marking questions, unit-by-unit slide decks, and short video lessons that students can watch on their phones.
